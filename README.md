# dermazone
dermazone helps users build confidence with their skin and helps them along their skincare journey. On the site, users can upload pictures of their face and have a convolutional neural network determine their skin type. Their result is then used to recommend dynamic and ranked skincare products for their current skin type. Users can also create an account to have their predictions saved.

Submitted to Ignition Hacks v.5.

To open the site, run 'main.py'.
